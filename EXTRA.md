# Ideas for extra features

- 1 Shields (drop from some defeated enemies, need to be picked up before they fall, can carry up to 9)
- 2 Sprint (right shoulder button, shift key)
  - Note: Shift and the right shoulder button already fire missiles, so sprint needs different
    buttons or missiles need to move.
- 3 Powerups (Speed, invincibility, fatter beam, super sprint)

## Player and weapons

- 4 Combo multiplier: quick kills in a row raise a score multiplier; it resets when you are hit
  or go too long without a kill.
- 5 Overheat laser: holding fire builds heat; letting it cool just before the limit gives a
  short burst of stronger shots.
- 6 Tractor beam: hold a button to pull a falling colonist or a dropped pickup toward the ship.
- 7 Wingman drone: a small helper that follows the ship and shoots; lost when you are hit.
- 8 Charged shot: hold fire with no targets to charge one piercing beam across the screen.
- 9 Decoy flare: drop a beacon that draws Wraiths and Stalkers away for a few seconds.

## Colonists and the planet

- 10 Carry several colonists: each one carried slows the ship slightly but multiplies the
  set-down bonus.
- 11 Colony buildings: a few domes on the surface; setting colonists down beside one earns more
  and, when full, the dome fires a ground turret at nearby Harvesters.
- 12 Colonist flares: a colonist being grabbed sends up a flare, shown on the scanner, so you
  know where to rush.
- 13 Refuge bunker: land briefly on a pad to repair one shield and restock a missile, at the
  cost of leaving the sky undefended.

## Enemies

- 14 Boss every fifth wave: a large mothership that crosses the planet launching Harvesters;
  it has weak points that must be shot in turn.
- 15 Jammer: an enemy that blanks part of the scanner until it is destroyed.
- 16 Miner: lays drifting mines along its path; mines can be shot for points or lured into
  other enemies.
- 17 Shielded Harvester: only vulnerable from behind or while it is lifting a colonist.
- 18 Splitter: breaks into two smaller, faster copies when shot.

## World and events

- 19 Meteor shower: a wave event where rocks rain down, hurting you and enemies alike.
- 20 Day and night: night dims the scene and shortens scanner range; enemies glow.
- 21 Solar storm: for a few seconds everything moves faster and score is doubled.
- 22 Different planets: after each rebuild, a new world with its own terrain shape, colours and
  gravity (ice, lava, low gravity moon).

## Modes and replay value

- 23 Daily challenge: the same seeded waves for everyone that day, with a shareable score.
- 24 Two players on one screen: a second ship on a second gamepad, sharing the colonists.
- 25 Survival mode: one life, endless waves, no rebuilds.
- 26 Medals: small goals such as "save every colonist in a wave" or "clear a wave with no
  bombs", shown on the title screen.
- 27 Upgrade shop between waves: spend rescued-colonist credits on one of three random
  upgrades.
- 28 Ghost replay: watch your best run flying alongside you as a faint ship.

## Feel

- 29 Slow motion for a moment on a last-second catch or the final kill of a wave.
- 30 Gamepad rumble and phone vibration on hits, bombs and catches.
- 31 Music that is generated in code like the sound effects and grows in layers as the wave
  heats up.
